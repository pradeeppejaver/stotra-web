Shiva

ಶ್ರೀ ಲಿಂಗಾಷ್ಟಕಂ

Shri Lingashtakam Stotram

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya

Font Size: 100%
View:
Verse 1

ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಮುರಾರಿ ಸುರಾರ್ಚಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ನಿರ್ಮಲಭಾಸಿತ ಶೋಭಿತ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ಜನ್ಮಜ ದುಃಖ ವಿನಾಶಕ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೧ ॥

brahmamurāri surārcita liṅgaṁ nirmalabhāsita śōbhita liṅgam ।
janmaja duḥkha vināśaka liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 1 ॥

I bow before that eternal Lingam of Lord Sadashiva, which is revered by Brahma, Vishnu (Murari), and the devas, pure and resplendently radiant, destroying all sorrows born of worldly births.

Verse 2

ದೇವಮುನಿ ಪ್ರವರಾರ್ಚಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಕಾಮದಹನ ಕರುಣಾಕರ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ರಾವಣ ದರ್ಪ ವಿನಾಶನ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೨ ॥

dēvamuni pravarārcita liṅgaṁ kāmadahana karuṇākara liṅgam ।
rāvaṇa darpa vināśana liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 2 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, worshipped by the noble gods and sages, which consumed Manmatha (desire/Kama), the ocean of compassion, and which crushed the pride of Ravana.

Verse 3

ಸರ್ವ ಸುಗಂಧ ಸುಲೇಪಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಬುದ್ಧಿ ವಿವರ್ಧನ ಕಾರಣ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ಸಿದ್ಧ ಸುರಾಸುರ ವಂದಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೩ ॥

sarva sugandha sulēpita liṅgaṁ buddhi vivardhana kāraṇa liṅgam ।
siddha surāsura vandita liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 3 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, anointed with fragrant perfumes, the prime cause for the expansion and sharpening of intellect, venerated by Siddhas, Devas, and Asuras alike.

Verse 4

ಕನಕ ಮಹಾಮಣಿ ಭೂಷಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಫಣಿಪತಿ ವೇಷ್ಟಿತ ಶೋಭಿತ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ದಕ್ಷಸುಯಜ್ಞ ವಿನಾಶನ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೪ ॥

kanaka mahāmaṇi bhūṣita liṅgaṁ phaṇipati vēṣṭita śōbhita liṅgam ।
dakṣasuyajña vināśana liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 4 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, adorned with gold and precious jewels, encircled and embellished by Vasuki (the King of Serpents), and which destroyed the sacrifice of Daksha.

Verse 5

ಕುಂಕುಮ ಚಂದನ ಲೇಪಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಪಂಕಜ ಹಾರ ಸುಶೋಭಿತ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ಸಂಚಿತ ಪಾಪ ವಿನಾಶನ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೫ ॥

kuṅkuma candana lēpita liṅgaṁ paṅkaja hāra suśōbhita liṅgam ।
sañcita pāpa vināśana liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 5 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, gracefully anointed with Kumkuma and Sandalwood paste, adorned with lotus garlands, and which destroys all accumulated karmic sins.

Verse 6

ದೇವಗಣಾರ್ಚಿತ ಸೇವಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಭಾವೈ-ರ್ಭಕ್ತಿಭಿರೇವ ಚ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ದಿನಕರ ಕೋಟಿ ಪ್ರಭಾಕರ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೬ ॥

dēvagaṇārcita sēvita liṅgaṁ bhāvair-bhaktibhirēva ca liṅgam ।
dinakara kōṭi prabhākara liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 6 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, served by hosts of devas with profound feelings of love and devotion, radiating with the brilliance of ten million suns.

Verse 7

ಅಷ್ಟದಳೋಪರಿವೇಷ್ಟಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಸರ್ವಸಮುದ್ಭವ ಕಾರಣ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ಅಷ್ಟದರಿದ್ರ ವಿನಾಶನ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೭ ॥

aṣṭadaḷōparivēṣṭita liṅgaṁ sarvasamudbhava kāraṇa liṅgam ।
aṣṭadaridra vināśana liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 7 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, seated upon an eight-petaled lotus, the prime cause of all creation, and which destroys the eight forms of poverty.

Verse 8

ಸುರಗುರು ಸುರವರ ಪೂಜಿತ ಲಿಂಗಂ ಸುರಾವನ ಪುಷ್ಪ ಸದಾರ್ಚಿತ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ।
ಪರಾತ್ಪರಂ ಪರಮಾತ್ಮಕ ಲಿಂಗಂ ತತ್ಪ್ರಣಮಾಮಿ ಸದಾಶಿವ ಲಿಂಗಮ್ ॥ ೮ ॥

suraguru suravara pūjita liṅgaṁ surāvana puṣpa sadārcita liṅgam ।
parātparaṁ paramātmaka liṅgaṁ tatpraṇamāmi sadāśiva liṅgam ॥ 8 ॥

I bow before that Sadashiva Lingam, worshipped by Brihaspati (guru of gods) and the highest devas, ever offered blossoms from heavenly gardens, the Supreme beyond Supreme, the Supreme Soul.

ಫಲಶೃತಿಃ | Phala Shruti

ಲಿಂಗಾಷ್ಟಕಮಿದಂ ಪುಣ್ಯಂ ಯಃ ಪಠೇಶ್ಶಿವ ಸನ್ನಿಧೌ ।
ಶಿವಲೋಕಮವಾಪ್ನೋತಿ ಶಿವೇನ ಸಹ ಮೋದತೇ ॥

liṅgāṣṭakamidaṁ puṇyaṁ yaḥ paṭhēśśiva sannidhau ।
śivalōkamavāpnōti śivēna saha mōdatē ॥

Whoever devoutly recites this sacred Lingashtakam in the holy presence of Lord Shiva attains the celestial abode of Shiva (Shivaloka) and resides in eternal bliss with Lord Shiva.
