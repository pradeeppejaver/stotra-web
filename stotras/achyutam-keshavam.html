ಅಚ್ಯುತಂ ಕೇಶವಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣ ದಾಮೋದರಂ

Achyutam Keshavam Krishna Damodaram

Opening Chant: Sri Adi Shankaracharya • Bhajan: Traditional Devotional

Font Size: 100%
View:
ಮಂತ್ರ | Chorus

ಅಚ್ಯುತಂ ಕೇಶವಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣ ದಾಮೋದರಂ ।
ರಾಮ ನಾರಾಯಣಂ ಜಾನಕೀ ವಲ್ಲಭಮ್ ॥

acyutaṁ kēśavaṁ kṛṣṇa dāmōdaraṁ ।
rāma nārāyaṇaṁ jānakī vallabham ॥

Achyuta (The Infallible), Keshava (Fine-haired), Krishna (All-attractive), Damodara (Tied with rope), Rama, Narayana, the beloved Lord of Janaki (Sita).

ಪದ್ಯ ೧ | Verse 1

ಕೌನ ಕಹತಾ ಹೈ ಭಗವಾನ ಆತೇ ನಹೀಂ ।
ತುಮ ಭಕ್ತ ಮೀರಾ ಕೇ ಜೈಸೇ ಬುಲಾತೇ ನಹೀಮ್ ॥ ೧ ॥

kauna kahatā hai bhagavāna ātē nahīṁ ।
tuma bhakta mīrā kē jaisē bulātē nahīṁ ॥ 1 ॥

Who says God does not come? You simply do not call Him with the pure devotion of Bhakta Meera!

ಪದ್ಯ ೨ | Verse 2

ಕೌನ ಕಹತಾ ಹೈ ಭಗವಾನ ಖಾತೇ ನಹೀಂ ।
ಬೇರ ಶಬರೀ ಕೇ ಜೈಸೇ ಖಿಲಾತೇ ನಹೀಮ್ ॥ ೨ ॥

kauna kahatā hai bhagavāna khātē nahīṁ ।
bēra śabarī kē jaisē khilātē nahīṁ ॥ 2 ॥

Who says God does not eat? You simply do not feed Him sweet berries with the selfless love of Shabari!

ಪದ್ಯ ೩ | Verse 3

ಕೌನ ಕಹತಾ ಹೈ ಭಗವಾನ ಸೋತೇ ನಹೀಂ ।
ಮಾ ಯಶೋದಾ ಕೇ ಜೈಸೇ ಸುಲಾತೇ ನಹೀಮ್ ॥ ೩ ॥

kauna kahatā hai bhagavāna sōtē nahīṁ ।
mā yaśōdā kē jaisē sulātē nahīṁ ॥ 3 ॥

Who says God does not sleep? You simply do not sing Him lullabies with the motherly affection of Yashoda!

ಪದ್ಯ ೪ | Verse 4

ಕೌನ ಕಹತಾ ಹೈ ಭಗವಾನ ನಾಚತೇ ನಹೀಂ ।
ಗೋಪಿಯೋಂ ಕೀ ತರಹ ತುಮ ನಚಾತೇ ನಹೀಮ್ ॥ ೪ ॥

kauna kahatā hai bhagavāna nācatē nahīṁ ।
gōpiyōṁ kī taraha tuma nacātē nahīṁ ॥ 4 ॥

Who says God does not dance? You simply do not make Him dance with the joyful devotion of the Gopis!

ಪದ್ಯ ೫ | Verse 5

ನಾಮ ಜಪತೇ ಚಲೋ ಕಾಮ ಕರತೇ ಚಲೋ ।
ಹರ ಸಮಯ ಕೃಷ್ಣ ಕಾ ಧ್ಯಾನ ಕರತೇ ಚಲೋ ॥ ೫ ॥

nāma japatē calō kāma karatē calō ।
hara samaya kṛṣṇa kā dhyāna karatē calō ॥ 5 ॥

Keep chanting the divine name while performing your daily work; keep meditating upon Lord Krishna at all times!

ಪದ್ಯ ೬ | Verse 6

ಯಾದ ಆಏಗೀ ಉನಕೋ ಕಭೀ ನಾ ಕಭೀ ।
ಕೃಷ್ಣ ದರ್ಶನ ತೋ ದೇಂಗೇ ಕಭೀ ನಾ ಕಭೀ ॥ ೬ ॥

yāda āēgī unakō kabhī nā kabhī ।
kṛṣṇa darśana tō dēṅgē kabhī nā kabhī ॥ 6 ॥

He will surely remember you sooner or later; Lord Krishna will grant His divine vision (Darshan) to you one day!
