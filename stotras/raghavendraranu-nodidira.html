ರಾಘವೇಂದ್ರರನು ನೋಡಿದಿರಾ

Raghavendraranu Nodidira Shira Bagi

Composed by Arun Rao Aroor • (ಅರುಣ್ ರಾವ್ ಆರೂರ್ - ಅಂಕಿತ: ಆರೂರು ವಿಠಲ)

Font Size: 100%
View:
ಪಲ್ಲವಿ | Pallavi

ರಾಘವೇಂದ್ರರನು ನೋಡಿದಿರಾ ।
ಶಿರಬಾಗಿ ವರಗಳನ್ನು ಬೇಡಿದಿರಾ ॥ ಪ ॥

rāghavēndraranu nōḍidirā ।
śirabāgi varagaḷannu bēḍidirā ॥ pa ॥

Have you beheld Sri Raghavendra? Have you bowed your head in devotion and prayed for his divine boons?

ಅನುಪಲ್ಲವಿ | Anupallavi

ಮಂತ್ರಾಲಯದಿ ನಿಂತು ಸಂತತ ಶ್ರೀಕಾಂತನ ।
ಸ್ತುತಿಸುವ ಸಂತಗುರು - ಶ್ರೀ ॥ ಅಪ ॥

mantrālayadi nintu santata śrīkāntana ।
stutisuva santaguru - śrī ॥ apa ॥

Dwelling eternally in holy Mantralaya, this revered saintly Guru continuously praises Lord Shrikanta (Lord Vishnu)!

ಚರಣ ೧ | Charanam 1

ಕಾಷಾಯ ವಸನ ಭೂಷಣನಾಗಿ ।
ದೋಷರಾಶಿಯ ನಾಶಗೊಳಿಸುವ - ಶ್ರೀ ॥ ೧ ॥

kāṣāya vasana bhūṣaṇanāgi ।
dōṣarāśiya nāśagoḷisuva - śrī ॥ 1 ॥

Adorned resplendently in sacred ochre robes, he destroys the heaps of sins and faults of his devotees!

ಚರಣ ೨ | Charanam 2

ದಂಡ ಕಮಂಡಲ ಕೈಯಲಿ ಹಿಡಿದು ।
ತೊಂಡರ ಪಾಪ ತಂಡವ ಕಳೆವ - ಶ್ರೀ ॥ ೨ ॥

daṇḍa kamaṇḍala kaiyali hiḍidu ।
toṇḍara pāpa taṇḍava kaḷeva - śrī ॥ 2 ॥

Holding the ascetic staff (Danda) and water pot (Kamandala) in hand, he eliminates the multitude of sins of humble servants!

ಚರಣ ೩ | Charanam 3

ಕೊರಳೊಳು ತುಳಸಿ ಸರವನು ಧರಿಸಿ ।
ವರದ ಆರೂರು ವಿಠಲನ ಸ್ಮರಿಪ - ಶ್ರೀ ॥ ೩ ॥

koraḷoḷu tuḷasi saravanu dharisi ।
varada ārūru viṭhalana smaripa - śrī ॥ 3 ॥

Wearing a holy garland of sacred Tulasi beads around his neck, he ever remembers and meditates upon the boon-giving Lord Arooru Vittala!
