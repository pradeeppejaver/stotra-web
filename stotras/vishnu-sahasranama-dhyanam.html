Vishnu

ಶ್ರೀ ವಿಷ್ಣು ಸಹಸ್ರನಾಮ ಧ್ಯಾನ ಶ್ಲೋಕಾಃ

Vishnu Sahasranama Dhyana Shlokas

From Mahabharata - Anushasana Parva

Font Size: 100%
View:
Dhyanam 1

ಕ್ಷೀರೋಧನ್ವತ್ಪ್ರದೇಶೇ ಶುಚಿಮಣಿವಿಲಸತ್ಸೈಕತೇಮೌಕ್ತಿಕಾನಾಂ
ಮಾಲಾಕ್ಲುಪ್ತಾಸನಸ್ಥಃ ಸ್ಫಟಿಕಮಣಿನಿಭೈರ್ಮೌಕ್ತಿಕೈರ್ಮಂಡಿತಾಂಗಃ ।
ಶುಭ್ರೈರಭ್ರೈರದಭ್ರೈರುಪರಿವಿರಚಿತೈರ್ಮುಕ್ತಪೀಯೂಷವರ್ಷಃ
ಆನಂದೀ ನಃ ಪುನೀಯಾದರಿನಳಿನಗದಾಶಂಖಪಾಣಿರ್ಮುಕುಂದಃ ॥ ೧ ॥

kṣīrōdhanvat-pradēśē śuci-maṇi-vilasat-saikatē-mauktikānāṁ
mālā-kluptāsanasthaḥ sphaṭika-maṇi-nibhair-mauktikair-maṇḍitāṅgaḥ ।
śubhrair-abhrair-adabhrair-upari-viracitair-mukta-pīyūṣa-varṣaḥ
ānandī naḥ punīyād-ari-naḷina-gadā-śaṁkha-pāṇir-mukundaḥ ॥ 1 ॥

In the region of the Ocean of Milk, seated upon a gem-encrusted throne, adorned with pearl necklaces glowing like pure crystal, showered by white celestial clouds pouring nectar. May Lord Mukunda—who holds the discus (chakra), lotus (padma), mace (gada), and conch (shankha) in His four hands—purify us all.

Dhyanam 2

ಭೂಃ ಪಾದೌ ಯಸ್ಯ ನಾಭಿರ್ವಿಯದಸುರನಿಲಶ್ಚಂದ್ರ ಸೂರ್ಯೌ ಚ ನೇತ್ರೇ
ಕರ್ಣಾವಾಶಾಃ ಶಿರೋ ದ್ಯೌರ್ಮುಖಮಪಿ ದಹನೋ ಯಸ್ಯ ವಾಸ್ತೇಯಮಬ್ಧಿಃ ।
ಅಂತಃಸ್ಥಂ ಯಸ್ಯ ವಿಶ್ವಂ ಸುರನರಖಗಗೋಭೋಗಿಗಂಧರ್ವದೈತ್ಯೈಃ
ಚಿತ್ರಂ ರಂರಮ್ಯತೇ ತಂ ತ್ರಿಭುವನವಪುಷಂ ವಿಷ್ಣುಮೀಶಂ ನಮಾಮಿ ॥ ೨ ॥

bhūḥ pādau yasya nābhir-viyad-asuranilaś-candra sūryau ca nētrē
karṇāv-āśāḥ śirō dyaur-mukham-api dahanō yasya vāstēyam-abdhiḥ ।
antaḥsthaṁ yasya viśvaṁ sura-nara-khaga-gō-bhōgi-gandharva-daityaiḥ
citraṁ raṁramyatē taṁ tribhuvana-vapuṣaṁ viṣṇum-īśaṁ namāmi ॥ 2 ॥

Whose feet are the earth, whose navel is the sky, whose eyes are the sun and moon, whose ears are the directions, whose head is the heavens, whose mouth is fire, and whose abdomen is the oceans. In whom the entire cosmos—gods, humans, birds, animals, serpents, gandharvas, and demons—resides. I bow to Lord Vishnu, whose cosmic body encompasses all three worlds.

Dhyanam 3

ಶಾಂತಾಕಾರಂ ಭುಜಗಶಯನಂ ಪದ್ಮನಾಭಂ ಸುರೇಶಂ
ವಿಶ್ವಾಧಾರಂ ಗಗನಸದೃಶಂ ಮೇಘವರ್ಣಂ ಶುಭಾಂಗಮ್ ।
ಲಕ್ಷ್ಮೀಕಾಂತಂ ಕಮಲನಯನಂ ಯೋಗಿಭಿರ್ಧ್ಯಾನಗಮ್ಯಂ
ವಂದೇ ವಿಷ್ಣುಂ ಭವಭಯಹರಂ ಸರ್ವಲೋಕೈಕನಾಥಮ್ ॥ ೩ ॥

śāntākāraṁ bhujaga-śayanaṁ padmanābhaṁ surēśaṁ
viśvādhāraṁ gagana-sadṛśaṁ mēghavarṇaṁ śubhāṅgam ।
lakṣmīkāntaṁ kamala-nayanaṁ yōgibhir-dhyānagamyaṁ
vandē viṣṇuṁ bhava-bhaya-haraṁ sarva-lōkai-ka-nātham ॥ 3 ॥

Whose form is serene, who rests upon the serpent Ananta, from whose navel sprouts the lotus, Lord of all gods, the foundation of the universe, boundless like the sky, dark-complexioned like clouds, of auspicious limbs. The beloved of Lakshmi, lotus-eyed, realized by yogis in meditation—I bow to Lord Vishnu who removes all fear of worldly existence.
