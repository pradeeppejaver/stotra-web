Hanuman • Stotra

ಶ್ರೀ ಹನುಮಾನ್ ಚಾಲೀಸಾ

Shri Hanuman Chalisa (Complete 40 Verses)

Composed by Sant Goswami Tulsidas (ಸಂತ ಗೋಸ್ವಾಮಿ ತುಳಸೀದಾಸರು)

Font Size: 100%
View:
ದೋಹಾ ೧ | Opening Doha 1

ಶ್ರೀ ಗುರು ಚರಣ ಸರೋಜ ರಜ ನಿಜಮನ ಮುಕುರ ಸುಧಾರಿ ।
ವರಣೌ ರಘುವರ ವಿಮಲಯಶ ಜೋ ದಾಯಕ ಫಲಚಾರಿ ॥

śrī guru caraṇa sarōja raja nijamana mukura sudhāri ।
varanau raghuvara vimalayaśa jō dāyaka phalacāri ॥

Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the lotus dust of Sri Guru's feet, I recount the pure glory of Sri Raghuvara, which bestows the four fruits of life (Dharma, Artha, Kama, Moksha).

ದೋಹಾ ೨ | Opening Doha 2

ಬುದ್ಧಿಹೀನ ತನುಜಾನಿಕೈ ಸುಮಿರೌ ಪವನ ಕುಮಾರ ।
ಬಲ ಬುದ್ಧಿ ವಿದ್ಯಾ ದೇಹು ಮೋಹಿ ಹರಹು ಕಲೇಶ ವಿಕಾರ ॥

buddhīhīna tanujānikai sumirau pavana kumāra ।
bala buddhi vidyā dēhu mōhi harahu kalēśa vikāra ॥

Knowing myself to be devoid of wisdom, I remember you, O Son of the Wind! Grant me strength, wisdom, and knowledge, and relieve me of all afflictions and flaws.

ಧ್ಯಾನಂ ೧ | Dhyanam 1

ಅತುಲಿತ ಬಲಧಾಮಂ ಸ್ವರ್ಣ ಶೈಲಾಭ ದೇಹಮ್ ।
ದನುಜ ವನ ಕೃಶಾನುಂ ಜ್ಞಾನಿನಾ-ಮಗ್ರಗಣ್ಯಮ್ ॥
ಸಕಲ ಗುಣ ನಿಧಾನಂ ವಾನರಾಣಾ-ಮಧೀಶಮ್ ।
ರಘುಪತಿ ಪ್ರಿಯ ಭಕ್ತಂ ವಾತಜಾತಂ ನಮಾಮಿ ॥

atulita baladhāmaṁ svarṇa śailābha dēham ।
danuja vana kṛśānum jñāninā-magragaṇyam ॥
sakala guṇa nidhānaṁ vānarāṇā-madhīśam ।
raghupati priya bhaktaṁ vātajātaṁ namāmi ॥

Abode of incomparable strength, whose body shines like a golden mountain, a fire to the forest of demons, foremost among the wise, storehouse of all virtues, lord of monkeys, beloved devotee of Lord Rama—I bow to the Son of the Wind.

ಧ್ಯಾನಂ ೨ | Dhyanam 2

ಗೋಷ್ಪದೀಕೃತ ವಾರಾಶಿಂ ಮಶಕೀಕೃತ ರಾಕ್ಷಸಮ್ ।
ರಾಮಾಯಣ ಮಹಾಮಾಲಾ ರತ್ನಂ ವಂದೇ-(ಅ)ನಿಲಾತ್ಮಜಮ್ ॥

gōṣpadīkṛta vārāśiṁ maśakīkṛta rākṣasam ।
rāmāyaṇa mahāmālā ratnaṁ vandē-'nilātmajam ॥

Who crossed the ocean as easily as stepping over a calf's footprint, and rendered mighty demons as insignificant as mosquitoes; I bow to the Son of the Wind, the luminous jewel in the great garland of Ramayana.

ಧ್ಯಾನಂ ೩ | Dhyanam 3

ಯತ್ರ ಯತ್ರ ರಘುನಾಥ ಕೀರ್ತನಂ ತತ್ರ ತತ್ರ ಕೃತಮಸ್ತಕಾಂಜಲಿಮ್ ।
ಭಾಷ್ಪವಾರಿ ಪರಿಪೂರ್ಣ ಲೋಚನಂ ಮಾರುತಿಂ ನಮತ ರಾಕ್ಷಸಾಂತಕಮ್ ॥

yatra yatra raghunātha kīrtanaṁ tatra tatra kṛtamastakāñjalim ।
bhāṣpavāri paripūrṇa lōcanaṁ mārutiṁ namata rākṣasāntakam ॥

Wherever the praises of Lord Raghunatha are sung, there Maruti stands with folded hands over his head and eyes filled with tears of devotion—I bow to Maruti, the destroyer of demons.

ಧ್ಯಾನಂ ೪ | Dhyanam 4

ಮನೋಜವಂ ಮಾರುತ ತುಲ್ಯವೇಗಮ್ ।
ಜಿತೇಂದ್ರಿಯಂ ಬುದ್ಧಿಮತಾಂ ವರಿಷ್ಠಮ್ ॥
ವಾತಾತ್ಮಜಂ ವಾನರಯೂಥ ಮುಖ್ಯಮ್ ।
ಶ್ರೀ ರಾಮ ದೂತಂ ಶಿರಸಾ ನಮಾಮಿ ॥

manōjavaṁ māruta tulyavēgam ।
jitēndriyaṁ buddhimatāṁ variṣtham ॥
vātātmajaṁ vānarayūtha mukhyam ।
śrī rāma dūtaṁ śirasā namāmi ॥

Swift as the mind and speed of the wind, master of the senses, supreme among the intelligent, son of the Wind God, chief of the monkey army, messenger of Sri Rama—I bow my head to you in reverence.

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧ | Chaupai 1

ಜಯ ಹನುಮಾನ ಜ್ಞಾನ ಗುಣ ಸಾಗರ ।
ಜಯ ಕಪೀಶ ತಿಹು ಲೋಕ ಉಜಾಗರ ॥ ೧ ॥

jaya hanumāna jñāna guṇa sāgara ।
jaya kapīśa tihu lōka ujāgara ॥ 1 ॥

Victory to Hanuman, ocean of knowledge and virtue! Victory to the Lord of monkeys, who illuminates all three worlds!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨ | Chaupai 2

ರಾಮದೂತ ಅತುಲಿತ ಬಲಧಾಮಾ ।
ಅಂಜನಿ ಪುತ್ರ ಪವನಸುತ ನಾಮಾ ॥ ೨ ॥

rāmadūta atulita baladhāmā ।
añjani putra pavanasuta nāmā ॥ 2 ॥

Messenger of Rama, abode of immeasurable strength, son of Anjani, known by the name of Pavanasuta!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩ | Chaupai 3

ಮಹಾವೀರ ವಿಕ್ರಮ ಬಜರಂಗೀ ।
ಕುಮತಿ ನಿವಾರ ಸುಮತಿ ಕೇ ಸಂಗೀ ॥ ೩ ॥

mahāvīra vikrama bajarangī ।
kumati nivāra sumati kē sangī ॥ 3 ॥

Great hero, immensely valiant, body strong as thunderbolt; dispeller of evil thoughts and companion of the wise!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೪ | Chaupai 4

ಕಂಚನ ವರಣ ವಿರಾಜ ಸುವೇಶಾ ।
ಕಾನನ ಕುಂಡಲ ಕುಂಚಿತ ಕೇಶಾ ॥ ೪ ॥

kañcana varaṇa virāja suvēśā ।
kānana kuṇḍala kuñcita kēśā ॥ 4 ॥

Golden-complexioned, handsomely attired, wearing ear ornaments and with curly locks of hair!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೫ | Chaupai 5

ಹಾಥವಜ್ರ ಔ ಧ್ವಜಾ ವಿರಾಜೈ ।
ಕಾಂಧೇ ಮೂಂಜ ಜನೇವೂ ಸಾಜೈ ॥ ೫ ॥

hāthavajra au dhvajā virājai ।
kāndhē mūñja janēvū sājai ॥ 5 ॥

Holding thunderbolt and flag in hands, adorned with the sacred thread of Munja grass across shoulders!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೬ | Chaupai 6

ಶಂಕರ ಸುವನ ಕೇಸರೀ ನಂದನ ।
ತೇಜ ಪ್ರತಾಪ ಮಹಾಜಗ ವಂದನ ॥ ೬ ॥

śaṅkara suvana kēsarī nandana ।
tēja pratāpa mahājaga vandana ॥ 6 ॥

Incarnation of Shiva, son of Kesari, whose radiant glory is revered by the entire universe!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೭ | Chaupai 7

ವಿದ್ಯಾವಾನ ಗುಣೀ ಅತಿ ಚಾತುರ ।
ರಾಮ ಕಾಜ ಕರಿವೇ ಕೋ ಆತುರ ॥ ೭ ॥

vidyāvāna guṇī ati cātura ।
rāma kāja karivē kō ātura ॥ 7 ॥

Highly learned, virtuous, extremely clever, ever eager to carry out Sri Rama's tasks!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೮ | Chaupai 8

ಪ್ರಭು ಚರಿತ್ರ ಸುನಿವೇ ಕೋ ರಸಿಯಾ ।
ರಾಮಲಖನ ಸೀತಾ ಮನ ಬಸಿಯಾ ॥ ೮ ॥

prabhu caritra sunivē kō nasiyā ।
rāmalakhana sītā mana basiyā ॥ 8 ॥

Delighting in listening to the divine deeds of the Lord, abiding forever in the hearts of Rama, Lakshmana, and Sita!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೯ | Chaupai 9

ಸೂಕ್ಷ್ಮ ರೂಪಧರಿ ಸಿಯಹಿ ದಿಖಾವಾ ।
ವಿಕಟ ರೂಪಧರಿ ಲಂಕ ಜಲಾವಾ ॥ ೯ ॥

sūkṣma rūpadhari siyahi dikhāvā ।
vikaṭa rūpadhari laṅka jalāvā ॥ 9 ॥

Assuming a subtle form to appear before Sita, assuming a formidable giant form to burn down Lanka!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೦ | Chaupai 10

ಭೀಮ ರೂಪಧರಿ ಅಸುರ ಸಂಹಾರೇ ।
ರಾಮಚಂದ್ರ ಕೇ ಕಾಜ ಸಂವಾರೇ ॥ ೧೦ ॥

bhīma rūpadhari asura saṁhārē ।
rāmacandra kē kāja saṁvārē ॥ 10 ॥

Assuming a colossal form to destroy the demons, fulfilling the divine mission of Lord Ramachandra!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೧ | Chaupai 11

ಲಾಯ ಸಂಜೀವನ ಲಖನ ಜಿಯಾಯೇ ।
ಶ್ರೀ ರಘುವೀರ ಹರಷಿ ಉರಲಾಯೇ ॥ ೧೧ ॥

lāya sañjīvana lakhana jiyāyē ।
śrī raghuvīra haraṣi uralāyē ॥ 11 ॥

Bringing the Sanjeevani herb to revive Lakshmana, filling Sri Raghuveera's heart with joy as He embraced you!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೨ | Chaupai 12

ರಘುಪತಿ ಕೀನ್ಹೀ ಬಹುತ ಬಡಾಯೀ ।
ತುಮ ಮಮ ಪ್ರಿಯ ಭರತ ಸಮ ಭಾಯೀ ॥ ೧೨ ॥

raghupati kīnhī bahuta baḍāyī ।
tuma mama priya bharata sama bhāyī ॥ 12 ॥

Lord Raghupati praised you immensely, saying 'You are as dear to Me as My own brother Bharata!'

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೩ | Chaupai 13

ಸಹಸ್ರ ವದನ ತುಮ್ಹರೋ ಯಶಗಾವೈ ।
ಅಸ ಕಹಿ ಶ್ರೀಪತಿ ಕಂಠ ಲಗಾವೈ ॥ ೧೩ ॥

sahasra vadana tumharō yaśagāvai ।
asa kahi śrīpati kaṇṭha lagāvai ॥ 13 ॥

'The thousand-headed Shesha sings your glory!'—saying this, the Lord of Sri embraced you around His neck.

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೪ | Chaupai 14

ಸನಕಾದಿಕ ಬ್ರಹ್ಮಾದಿ ಮುನೀಶಾ ।
ನಾರದ ಶಾರದ ಸಹಿತ ಅಹೀಶಾ ॥ ೧೪ ॥

sanakādika brahmādi munīśā ।
nārada śārada sahita ahīśā ॥ 14 ॥

Sanaka and ancient sages, Brahma and gods, Narada, Goddess Saraswati, and the King of Serpents sing your praise!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೫ | Chaupai 15

ಯಮ ಕುಬೇರ ದಿಗಪಾಲ ಜಹಾಂ ತೇ ।
ಕವಿ ಕೋವಿದ ಕಹಿ ಸಕೇ ಕಹಾಂ ತೇ ॥ ೧೫ ॥

yama kubēra digapāla jahāṁ tē ।
kavi kōvida kahi sakē kahāṁ tē ॥ 15 ॥

Yama, Kubera, and guardians of directions—how can poets and scholars fully describe your limitless glory?

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೬ | Chaupai 16

ತುಮ ಉಪಕಾರ ಸುಗ್ರೀವಹಿ ಕೀನ್ಹಾ ।
ರಾಮ ಮಿಲಾಯ ರಾಜಪದ ದೀನ್ಹಾ ॥ ೧೬ ॥

tuma upakāra sugrīvahi kīnhā ।
rāma milāya rājapada dīnhā ॥ 16 ॥

You rendered immense help to Sugriva by introducing him to Lord Rama and bestowing the royal kingdom upon him!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೭ | Chaupai 17

ತುಮ್ಹರೋ ಮಂತ್ರ ವಿಭೀಷಣ ಮಾನಾ ।
ಲಂಕೇಶ್ವರ ಭಯೇ ಸಬ ಜಗ ಜಾನಾ ॥ ೧೭ ॥

tumharō mantra vibhīṣaṇa mānā ।
laṅkēśvara bhayē saba jaga jānā ॥ 17 ॥

Vibhishana heeded your counsel and became the King of Lanka, as known to the whole world!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೮ | Chaupai 18

ಯುಗ ಸಹಸ್ರ ಯೋಜನ ಪರ ಭಾನೂ ।
ಲೀಲ್ಯೋ ತಾಹಿ ಮಧುರ ಫಲ ಜಾನೂ ॥ ೧೮ ॥

yuga sahasra yōjana para bhānū ।
līlyō tāhi madhura phala jānū ॥ 18 ॥

You leaped thousands of Yojanas to swallow the Sun, mistaking it for a sweet fruit!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೧೯ | Chaupai 19

ಪ್ರಭು ಮುದ್ರಿಕಾ ಮೇಲಿ ಮುಖ ಮಾಹೀ ।
ಜಲಧಿ ಲಾಂಘಿ ಗಯೇ ಅಚರಜ ನಾಹೀ ॥ ೧೯ ॥

prabhu mudrikā mēli mukha māhī ।
jaladhi lāṅghi gayē acaraja nāhī ॥ 19 ॥

Holding the Lord's signet ring in your mouth, you leaped across the vast ocean—no wonder for you!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೦ | Chaupai 20

ದುರ್ಗಮ ಕಾಜ ಜಗತ ಕೇ ಜೇತೇ ।
ಸುಗಮ ಅನುಗ್ರಹ ತುಮ್ಹರೇ ತೇತೇ ॥ ೨೦ ॥

durgama kāja jagata kē jētē ।
sugama anugraha tumharē tētē ॥ 20 ॥

Whatever difficult tasks exist in the world become easy through your divine grace!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೧ | Chaupai 21

ರಾಮ ದುಆರೇ ತುಮ ರಖವಾರೇ ।
ಹೋತ ನ ಆಜ್ಞಾ ಬಿನು ಪೈಸಾರೇ ॥ ೨೧ ॥

rāma duārē tuma rakhavārē ।
hōta na ājñā binu paisārē ॥ 21 ॥

You are the guardian at Rama's gateway; no one can enter without your permission!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೨ | Chaupai 22

ಸಬ ಸುಖ ಲಹೈ ತುಮ್ಹಾರೀ ಶರಣಾ ।
ತುಮ ರಕ್ಷಕ ಕಾಹೂ ಕೋ ಡರ ನಾ ॥ ೨೨ ॥

saba sukha lahai tumhārī śaraṇā ।
tuma rakṣaka kāhū kō ḍara nā ॥ 22 ॥

All happiness is attained under your refuge; with you as protector, there is no fear!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೩ | Chaupai 23

ಆಪನ ತೇಜ ಸಮ್ಹಾರೋ ಆಪೈ ।
ತೀನೋಂ ಲೋಕ ಹಾಂಕ ತೇ ಕಾಂಪೈ ॥ ೨೩ ॥

āpana tēja samhāro āpai ।
tīnōṁ lōka hāṅka tē kāmpai ॥ 23 ॥

You alone can control your immense radiance; all three worlds tremble at your roar!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೪ | Chaupai 24

ಭೂತ ಪಿಶಾಚ ನಿಕಟ ನಹಿ ಆವೈ ।
ಮಹವೀರ ಜಬ ನಾಮ ಸುನಾವೈ ॥ ೨೪ ॥

bhūta piśāca nikaṭa nahi āvai ।
mahavīra jaba nāma sunāvai ॥ 24 ॥

Ghosts and evil spirits cannot come near when the name of Mahaveer Hanuman is uttered!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೫ | Chaupai 25

ನಾಸೈ ರೋಗ ಹರೈ ಸಬ ಪೀರಾ ।
ಜಪತ ನಿರಂತರ ಹನುಮತ ವೀರಾ ॥ ೨೫ ॥

nāsai rōga harai saba pīrā ।
japata nirantara hanumata vīrā ॥ 25 ॥

All diseases are cured and all pains destroyed by constantly chanting the name of brave Hanuman!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೬ | Chaupai 26

ಸಂಕಟ ಸೇ ಹನುಮಾನ ಛುಡಾವೈ ।
ಮನ ಕ್ರಮ ವಚನ ಧ್ಯಾನ ಜೋ ಲಾವೈ ॥ ೨೬ ॥

saṅkaṭa sē hanumāna chuḍāvai ।
mana krama vacana dhyāna jō lāvai ॥ 26 ॥

Hanuman rescues from all troubles those who meditate upon Him in thought, word, and deed!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೭ | Chaupai 27

ಸಬ ಪರ ರಾಮ ತಪಸ್ವೀ ರಾಜಾ ।
ತಿನಕೇ ಕಾಜ ಸಕಲ ತುಮ ಸಾಜಾ ॥ ೨೭ ॥

saba para rāma tapasvī rājā ।
tinakē kāja sakala tuma sājā ॥ 27 ॥

Sri Rama is the supreme ascetic King over all, and you accomplished all His divine endeavors!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೮ | Chaupai 28

ಔರ ಮನೋರಥ ಜೋ ಕೋಯಿ ಲಾವೈ ।
ತಾಸು ಅಮಿತ ಜೀವನ ಫಲ ಪಾವೈ ॥ ೨೮ ॥

aura manōratha jō kōyi lāvai ।
tāsu amita jīvana phala pāvai ॥ 28 ॥

Whoever brings any righteous desire to you attains the boundless fruit of human life!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೨೯ | Chaupai 29

ಚಾರೋ ಯುಗ ಪ್ರತಾಪ ತುಮ್ಹಾರಾ ।
ಹೈ ಪರಸಿದ್ಧ ಜಗತ ಉಜಿಯಾರಾ ॥ ೨೯ ॥

cārō yuga pratāpa tumhārā ।
hai parasiddha jagata ujiyārā ॥ 29 ॥

Your glory resounds across all four Yugas, renowned throughout the world as a radiant light!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೦ | Chaupai 30

ಸಾಧು ಸಂತ ಕೇ ತುಮ ರಖವಾರೇ ।
ಅಸುರ ನಿಕಂದನ ರಾಮ ದುಲಾರೇ ॥ ೩೦ ॥

sādhu santa kē tuma rakhavārē ।
asura nikandana rāma dulārē ॥ 30 ॥

Protector of saints and sages, destroyer of demons, beloved of Sri Rama!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೧ | Chaupai 31

ಅಷ್ಟಸಿದ್ಧಿ ನವ ನಿಧಿ ಕೇ ದಾತಾ ।
ಅಸ ವರ ದೀನ್ಹ ಜಾನಕೀ ಮಾತಾ ॥ ೩೧ ॥

aṣṭasiddhi nava nidhi kē dātā ।
asa vara dīnha jānakī mātā ॥ 31 ॥

Bestower of the eight Siddhis and nine Nidhis—this boon was granted to you by Mother Janaki!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೨ | Chaupai 32

ರಾಮ ರಸಾಯನ ತುಮ್ಹಾರೇ ಪಾಸಾ ।
ಸದಾ ರಹೋ ರಘುಪತಿ ಕೇ ದಾಸಾ ॥ ೩೨ ॥

rāma rasāyana tumhārē pāsā ।
sadā rahō raghupati kē dāsā ॥ 32 ॥

You possess the divine elixir of Rama's devotion; may you ever remain the humble servant of Raghupati!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೩ | Chaupai 33

ತುಮ್ಹರೇ ಭಜನ ರಾಮಕೋ ಪಾವೈ ।
ಜನ್ಮ ಜನ್ಮ ಕೇ ದುಖ ಬಿಸರಾವೈ ॥ ೩೩ ॥

tumharē bhajana rāmakō pāvai ।
janma janma kē dukha bisarāvai ॥ 33 ॥

Chanting your devotion leads to Sri Rama, erasing the sorrows of countless past births!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೪ | Chaupai 34

ಅಂತ ಕಾಲ ರಘುಪತಿ ಪುರಜಾಯೀ ।
ಜಹಾಂ ಜನ್ಮ ಹರಿಭಕ್ತ ಕಹಾಯೀ ॥ ೩೪ ॥

anta kāla raghupati purajāyī ।
jahāṁ janma haribhakta kahāyī ॥ 34 ॥

At the end of life, one enters the divine abode of Raghupati and is remembered as a true devotee of Lord Hari!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೫ | Chaupai 35

ಔರ ದೇವತಾ ಚಿತ್ತ ನ ಧರಯೀ ।
ಹನುಮತ ಸೇಯಿ ಸರ್ವ ಸುಖ ಕರಯೀ ॥ ೩೫ ॥

aura dēvatā citta na dharayī ।
hanumata sēyi sarva sukha karayī ॥ 35 ॥

Without needing any other deity in mind, serving Hanuman alone bestows all joy and peace!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೬ | Chaupai 36

ಸಂಕಟ ಕಟೈ ಮಿಟೈ ಸಬ ಪೀರಾ ।
ಜೋ ಸುಮಿರೈ ಹನುಮತ ಬಲ ವೀರಾ ॥ ೩೬ ॥

saṅkaṭa kaṭai miṭai saba pīrā ।
jō sumirai hanumata bala vīrā ॥ 36 ॥

All troubles vanish and all agony ends for one who remembers the mighty brave Hanuman!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೭ | Chaupai 37

ಜೈ ಜೈ ಜೈ ಹನುಮಾನ ಗೋಸಾಯೀ ।
ಕೃಪಾ ಕರಹು ಗುರುದೇವ ಕೀ ನಾಯೀ ॥ ೩೭ ॥

jai jai jai hanumāna gōsāyī ।
kṛpā karahu gurudēva kī nāyī ॥ 37 ॥

Hail, hail, hail to Lord Hanuman! Shower your grace upon us like a divine spiritual Guru!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೮ | Chaupai 38

ಯಹ ಶತ ವಾರ ಪಾಠ ಕರ ಕೋಯೀ ।
ಛೂಟಹಿ ಬಂದಿ ಮಹಾ ಸುಖ ಹೋಯೀ ॥ ೩೮ ॥

yaha śata vāra pāṭha kara kōyī ।
chūṭahi bandi mahā sukha hōyī ॥ 38 ॥

Whoever recites this Hanuman Chalisa a hundred times is freed from worldly bondage and attains supreme bliss!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೩೯ | Chaupai 39

ಜೋ ಯಹ ಪಡೈ ಹನುಮಾನ ಚಾಲೀಸಾ ।
ಹೋಯ ಸಿದ್ಧಿ ಸಾಖೀ ಗೌರೀಶಾ ॥ ೩೯ ॥

jō yaha paḍai hanumāna cālīsā ।
hōya siddhi sākhī gaurīśā ॥ 39 ॥

Whoever reads this Hanuman Chalisa attains all perfection—Lord Shiva Himself bears witness to this!

ಚೌಪಾಈ ೪೦ | Chaupai 40

ತುಲಸೀದಾಸ ಸದಾ ಹರಿ ಚೇರಾ ।
ಕೀಜೈ ನಾಥ ಹೃದಯ ಮಹ ಡೇರಾ ॥ ೪೦ ॥

tulasīdāsa sadā hari cērā ।
kījai nātha hṛdaya maha ḍērā ॥ 40 ॥

Tulsidas is ever the servant of Lord Hari; O Lord Hanuman, reside forever within my heart!

ದೋಹಾ (ಸಮಾಪ್ತಿ) | Closing Doha

ಪವನ ತನಯ ಸಂಕಟ ಹರಣ ಮಂಗಳ ಮೂರತಿ ರೂಪ್ ।
ರಾಮ ಲಖನ ಸೀತಾ ಸಹಿತ ಹೃದಯ ಬಸಹು ಸುರಭೂಪ್ ॥

pavana tanaya saṅkaṭa haraṇa maṅgaḷa mūrati rūp ।
rāma lakhana sītā sahita hṛdaya basahu surabhūp ॥

O Son of the Wind, remover of afflictions, embodiment of all auspiciousness! Reside in my heart along with Sri Rama, Lakshmana, and Sita, O King of Gods!

ಜಯಘೋಷ | Chanting of Victory

ಸಿಯಾವರ ರಾಮಚಂದ್ರಕೀ ಜಯ ।
ಪವನಸುತ ಹನುಮಾನಕೀ ಜಯ ।
ಬೋಲೋ ಭಾಯೀ ಸಬ ಸಂತನಕೀ ಜಯ ॥

siyāvara rāmacandrakī jaya ।
pavanasuta hanumānakī jaya ।
bōlō bhāyī saba santanakī jaya ॥

Victory to Lord Ramachandra, consort of Sita! Victory to Hanuman, Son of the Wind! Hail to all saints and divine devotees!

ಮಂಗಲ ಶ್ಲೋಕ ೧ | Closing Shloka 1

ಮನೋಜವಂ ಮಾರುತತುಲ್ಯವೇಗಂ ಜಿತೇಂದ್ರಿಯಂ ಬುದ್ಧಿಮತಾಂ ವರಿಷ್ಠಮ್ ।
ವಾತಾತ್ಮಜಂ ವಾನರಯೂಥಮುಖ್ಯಂ ಶ್ರೀರಾಮದೂತಂ ಶರಣಂ ಪ್ರಪದ್ಯೇ ॥

manōjavaṁ mārutatulyavēgaṁ jitēndriyaṁ buddhimatāṁ variṣṭham ।
vātātmajaṁ vānarayūthamukhyaṁ śrīrāmadūtaṁ śaraṇaṁ prapadyē ॥

I take refuge in Sri Rama's messenger Hanuman—who is swift as thought and wind, who has mastered his senses, who is foremost among the wise, son of the Wind God, and leader of the monkey army!

ಮಂಗಲ ಶ್ಲೋಕ ೨ | Closing Shloka 2

ಅಂಜನೇಯ ಮತಿಪಾಟಲಾನನಂ ಕಾಂಚನಾದ್ರಿ ಕಮನೀಯ ವಿಗ್ರಹಮ್ ।
ಪಾರಿಜಾತ ತರು ಮೂಲವಾಸಿನಂ ಭಾವಯಾಮಿ ಪವಮಾನ ನಂದನಮ್ ॥

añjanēya matipāṭalānanaṁ kāñcanādri kamanīya vigraham ।
pārijāta taru mūlavāsinaṁ bhāvayāmi pavamāna nandanam ॥

I meditate upon Anjaneya, the son of the Wind God, whose face glows with intelligence, whose charming form shines like a golden mountain, and who resides at the foot of the celestial Parijata tree!
