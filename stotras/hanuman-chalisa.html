Hanuman

ಶ್ರೀ ಹನುಮಾನ್ ಚಾಲೀಸಾ

Shri Hanuman Chalisa

Composed by Goswami Tulsidas

Font Size: 100%
View:
Opening Doha 1

ಶ್ರೀಗುರು ಚರನ ಸರೋಜ ರಜ ನಿಜ ಮನು ಮುಕುರು ಸುಧಾರಿ ।
ಬರನಂ ರಘುಬರ ಬಿಮಲ ಜಸು ಜೊ ದಾಯಕು ಫಲ ಚಾರಿ ॥

śrīguru carana sarōja raja nija manu mukuru sudhāri ।
baranaṁ raghubara bimala jasu jo dāyaku phala cāri ॥

Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust from the lotus feet of the Divine Guru, I sing the pure glory of Lord Rama, which bestows the four fruits of life (Dharma, Artha, Kama, and Moksha).

Opening Doha 2

ಬುದ್ಧಿಹೀನ ತನು ಜಾನಿಕೇ ಸುಮಿರೆ ಪವನಕುಮಾರ ।
ಬಲ ಬುದ್ಧಿ ಬಿಧ್ಯಾ ದೇಹು ಮೋಹಿ ಹರಹು ಕಲೇಸ ಬಿಕಾರ ॥

buddhihīna tanu jānikē sumirē pavanakumāra ।
bala buddhi bidhyā dēhu mōhi harahu kalēsa bikāra ॥

Knowing myself to be devoid of wisdom, I remember you, O Son of the Wind God (Pavanakumara). Grant me physical strength, sharp intelligence, and spiritual knowledge, and relieve me of all afflictions and impurities.

Chaupai 1

ಜಯ ಹನುಮಾನ ಜ್ಞಾನ ಗುಣ ಸಾಗರ ।
ಜಯ ಕಪೀಶ ತಿಹುಂ ಲೋಕ ಉಜಾಗರ ॥ ೧ ॥

jaya hanumāna jñāna guṇa sāgara ।
jaya kapīśa tihuṁ lōka ujāgara ॥ 1 ॥

Victory to Hanuman, who is an ocean of wisdom and virtues! Victory to the Lord of Monkeys, who illuminates all the three worlds!

Chaupai 2

ರಾಮದೂತ ಅತುಲಿತ ಬಲ ಧಾಮಾ ।
ಅಂಜನಿಪುತ್ರ ಪವನಸುತ ನಾಮಾ ॥ ೨ ॥

rāmadūta atulita bala dhāmā ।
añjaniputra pavanasuta nāmā ॥ 2 ॥

You are the messenger of Lord Rama, the abode of incomparable strength, known as Anjaniputra (son of Anjana) and Pavanasuta (son of the Wind).
