Vishnu

ಶ್ರೀ ಕೃಷ್ಣಾಷ್ಟಕಂ

Shri Krishnashtakam Stotram

Composed by Sri Adi Shankaracharya

Font Size: 100%
View:
Verse 1

ವಸುದೇವ ಸುತಂ ದೇವಂ ಕಂಸ ಚಾಣೂರ ಮರ್ದನಮ್ ।
ದೇವಕೀ ಪರಮಾನಂದಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೧ ॥

vasudēva sutaṁ dēvaṁ kaṁsa cāṇūra mardanam ।
dēvakī paramānandaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 1 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the entire universe, the divine son of Vasudeva, the slayer of Kamsa and Chanura, and the supreme joy of Mother Devaki.

Verse 2

ಅತಸೀ ಪುಷ್ಪ ಸಂಕಾಶಂ ಹಾರ ನೂಪುರ ಶೋಭಿತಮ್ ।
ರತ್ನ ಕಂಕಣ ಕೇಯೂರಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೨ ॥

atasī puṣpa saṁkāśaṁ hāra nūpura śōbhitam ।
ratna kaṅkaṇa kēyūraṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 2 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, who shines like the blue Atasi flower, adorned with sparkling necklaces, anklets, jeweled bangles, and armlets.

Verse 3

ಕುಟಿಲಾಲಕ ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಂ ಪೂರ್ಣಚಂದ್ರ ನಿಭಾನನಮ್ ।
ವಿಲಸತ್ ಕುಂಡಲಧರಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರಮ್ ॥ ೩ ॥

kuṭilālaka saṁyuktaṁ pūrṇacandra nibhānanam ।
vilasat kuṇḍaladharaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 3 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, graced with dark curly locks, whose face shines like the full moon, and who wears brilliant earrings.

Verse 4

ಮಂದಾರ ಗಂಧ ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಂ ಚಾರುಹಾಸಂ ಚತುರ್ಭುಜಮ್ ।
ಬರ್ಹಿ ಪಿಂಛಾವ ಚೂಡಾಂಗಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೪ ॥

mandāra gandha saṁyuktaṁ cāruhāsaṁ caturbhujam ।
barhi piṁchāva cūḍāṅgaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 4 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, fragrant with Mandara flowers, possessing a sweet gentle smile, four-armed, and wearing a peacock feather on his crown.

Verse 5

ಉತ್ಫುಲ್ಲ ಪದ್ಮಪತ್ರಾಕ್ಷಂ ನೀಲ ಜೀಮೂತ ಸನ್ನಿಭಮ್ ।
ಯಾದವಾನಾಂ ಶಿರೋರತ್ನಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೫ ॥

utphulla padmapatrākṣaṁ nīla jīmūta sannibham ।
yādavānāṁ śirōratnaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 5 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, whose eyes resemble blooming lotus petals, whose body is dark blue like a rain cloud, and who is the crest jewel of the Yadavas.

Verse 6

ರುಕ್ಮಿಣೀ ಕೇಳಿ ಸಂಯುಕ್ತಂ ಪೀತಾಂಬರ ಸುಶೋಭಿತಮ್ ।
ಅವಾಪ್ತ ತುಲಸೀ ಗಂಧಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೬ ॥

rukmiṇī kēḷi saṁyuktaṁ pītāmbara suśōbhitam ।
avāpta tulasī gandhaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 6 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, engaged in joyful divine play with Rukmini, resplendent in yellow silk robes, and fragrant with sacred Tulsi leaves.

Verse 7

ಗೋಪಿಕಾನಾಂ ಕುಚದ್ವಂದ ಕುಂಕುಮಾಂಕಿತ ವಕ್ಷಸಮ್ ।
ಶ್ರೀನಿಕೇತಂ ಮಹೇಷ್ವಾಸಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೭ ॥

gōpikānāṁ kucadvanda kuṁkumāṅkita vakṣasam ।
śrīnikētaṁ mahēṣvāsaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 7 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, whose chest is marked with Kumkuma, who is the abode of Sri (Lakshmi), and the wielders of mighty bows.

Verse 8

ಶ್ರೀವತ್ಸಾಂಕಂ ಮಹೋರಸ್ಕಂ ವನಮಾಲಾ ವಿರಾಜಿತಮ್ ।
ಶಂಖಚಕ್ರ ಧರಂ ದೇವಂ ಕೃಷ್ಣಂ ವಂದೇ ಜಗದ್ಗುರುಮ್ ॥ ೮ ॥

śrīvatsāṅkaṁ mahōraskaṁ vanamālā virājitam ।
śaṁkhacakra dharaṁ dēvaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ vandē jagadgurum ॥ 8 ॥

I bow to Lord Krishna, the Guru of the universe, bearing the sacred Srivatsa mark on his broad chest, wearing the Vanamala garland of wildflowers, holding the Conch and Discus.

ಫಲಶೃತಿಃ | Phala Shruti

ಕೃಷ್ಣಾಷ್ಟಕ ಮಿದಂ ಪುಣ್ಯಂ ಪ್ರಾತರುತ್ಥಾಯ ಯಃ ಪಠೇತ್ ।
ಕೋಟಿಜನ್ಮ ಕೃತಂ ಪಾಪಂ ಸ್ಮರಣೇನ ವಿನಶ್ಯತಿ ॥

kṛṣṇāṣṭaka midaṁ puṇyaṁ prātarutthāya yaḥ paṭhēt ।
kōṭijanma kṛtaṁ pāpaṁ smaraṇēna vinaśyati ॥

Whoever devoutly recites this holy Krishnashtakam upon waking up in the morning is liberated from sins committed across ten million births simply by remembering Lord Krishna.
